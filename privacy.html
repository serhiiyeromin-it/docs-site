Privacy Policy

This application is a personal tool used solely by its owner to upload and publish the owner's own video content to the owner's own TikTok account(s) via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The application stores only the OAuth access token and refresh token required to authenticate with TikTok's API on behalf of the owner. No data belonging to any other user is collected, stored, or shared. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Tokens are stored locally and can be deleted by the owner at any time by disconnecting the app from their TikTok account settings.
